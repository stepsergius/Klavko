Thank you for buying Klavko Pro!

Your license key is on the order page and in the receipt email.

Activate Pro in three steps

  1. Copy your license key from the receipt email.
  2. Open Klavko's settings: click the Klavko icon in the menu bar, choose Settings, then open Klavko Pro.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate. All Pro features work right away.

Don't have Klavko yet?

Download Klavko for Mac. It needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Using more than one Mac?

Your key works on up to 2 Macs. To move it to another Mac, click Deactivate on This Mac on the Klavko Pro page in Klavko's settings.

Need help?

Didn't get the email, or the key doesn't activate? Write to stepsergius@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.
